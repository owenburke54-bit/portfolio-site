# Owen — Soccer

## Youth sports

Owen grew up playing soccer, basketball, lacrosse, and baseball (when younger). He stopped lacrosse after 7th grade and basketball after 8th grade to focus on soccer for college.

## Club history

- **Abbey Villa Soccer Club** — roughly grades 2–6  
- **NEFC** — started around 6th grade; began on the B team, trained with the A team at times, later moved to the top team; stayed through junior year of high school

## Position and style

Preferred position is **8** (central midfielder).

## College adjustment

- Spacing and making room for teammates  
- Not always dropping back for the ball  
- Moving less can help — staying still or drifting can improve team flow  
- Playing smarter in college vs. a more ball-dominant high school style
